Currently working on

Privacy-aware learning for actions the model has never seen.

My current Ph.D. research brings together federated optimization and zero-shot learning to recognize human actions from distributed skeleton data.

01

Zero-shot skeleton action recognition

Learning transferable visual-semantic representations so models can identify unseen action classes without direct training examples from those classes.

Federated, privacy-preserving learning

Training across distributed clients while keeping skeleton features local, sharing model updates rather than private training data.

FedAvgNon-IID clients

Communication-aware optimization

Studying iteration-based aggregation and local-versus-global alignment losses to improve accuracy under heterogeneous client distributions.
